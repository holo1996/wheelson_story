## 2026-06-21 - LCP Bottleneck: Unoptimized Video Posters
**Learning:** Video posters in PNG format can be excessively large (4MB+), severely impacting the Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) and overall page load speed.
**Action:** Convert heavy PNG posters to optimized JPEGs (target ~200KB) and resize to a maximum width of 1280px to balance quality and performance.
